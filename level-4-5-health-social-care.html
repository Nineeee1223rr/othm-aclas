Level 4 & 5 Diploma in Health and Social Care Management

240 credits (120 per level) | 12-18 months | Equivalent to Years 1 & 2 of a UK Bachelor's Degree | 100% Online

Overview

The OTHM Level 4 and Level 5 Diploma in Health and Social Care Management provides a comprehensive foundation for a career in health, social care, and community settings. Equivalent to the first two years of a UK bachelor's degree, this combined programme equips you with the knowledge and skills to manage effectively in health and care environments.

What You Will Learn

Level 4 units

  • Promoting Equality, Diversity and Inclusion in Health and Social Care
  • Professional Development and Academic Writing Skills
  • Communication in the Caring Professions
  • Principles of Health and Safety for Health Professions
  • Assessment Processes in Health and Social Care Settings
  • Resource Management in Health and Social Care

Level 5 units

  • Working in Partnership in Health and Social Care
  • Managing the Safeguarding and Protection of Vulnerable Individuals
  • Health Education and Promoting Wellbeing
  • Team Management in Health and Social Care
  • Professional Supervision Practice in Health and Social Care
  • Research Methods in Health and Social Care

Unit titles are as published by OTHM. The full unit table with official references and credit values is below.

Study Hours, Entry and Assessment

Guided learning hours (GLH): 600 per level (minimum)
Total qualification time (TQT): 1,200 per level
Entry requirements: Level 4: typically aged 18 or over, with a relevant Level 3 Diploma (or equivalent), GCE Advanced level in 2 subjects (or equivalent), or — for mature learners over 21 — relevant management experience. Level 5: a relevant Level 4 Diploma (or equivalent), GCE Advanced level in 3 subjects (or equivalent), or mature entry over 21. English language competency is required if English is not your first language.
Assessment: All units are internally assessed by the centre and externally verified by OTHM. Assessment is coursework-based — written assignments against published criteria, with no examinations. Grading is Pass/Fail.
Programme status: This is a prospective programme, subject to OTHM centre approval. Enrolment will open once approval is granted.

Progression Routes

  • Top up to a full Bachelor's degree - Final-year entry to a BA (Hons) Health and Social Care at a UK university.
  • Enter management roles - Pursue positions in care homes, hospitals, community health organisations, or social services.
  • Continue to postgraduate study - Progress to Level 6 or Level 7 qualifications for further specialisation.
