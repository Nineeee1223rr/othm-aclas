Level 6 Diploma in Business Management

120 credits | 9-12 months | Equivalent to the Final Year of a UK Bachelor's Degree | 100% Online

Overview

The OTHM Level 6 Diploma in Business Management is a graduate-level qualification, equivalent to the final year of a UK bachelor's degree. It is designed for learners who already hold a Level 5 qualification (or equivalent) and want to complete a full degree-level diploma or prepare for postgraduate study.

What You Will Learn

Mandatory units

  • Leadership and People Management
  • Business Research Project
  • Operations Management
  • Financial Decision Making
  • Sustainable Business Practices
  • Strategic Human Resource Management

Unit titles are as published by OTHM. The full unit table with official references and credit values is below.

Study Hours, Entry and Assessment

Guided learning hours (GLH): 480
Total qualification time (TQT): 1,200
Entry requirements: Typically aged 18 or over, with a relevant RQF Level 5 Diploma (or equivalent). Mature learners over 21 with relevant management experience may also be considered. English language competency is required if English is not your first language.
Assessment: All units are internally assessed by the centre and externally verified by OTHM. Assessment is coursework-based — written assignments against published criteria, with no examinations. Grading is Pass/Fail.
Programme status: This is a prospective programme, subject to OTHM centre approval. Enrolment will open once approval is granted.

Progression Routes

  • Honours-degree equivalence - The 120 Level 6 credits are equivalent to the final year of a UK bachelor's honours degree.
  • Progress to postgraduate study - Qualify for entry to OTHM Level 7 or a Master's programme.
  • Career advancement - Demonstrate graduate-level knowledge to current or prospective employers.
