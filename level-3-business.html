Level 3 Diploma in Business Studies

60 credits | 6-9 months | A-Level / High School Equivalent | 100% Online

Overview

The OTHM Level 3 Diploma in Business Studies is an introductory qualification designed for school leavers, career changers, and anyone new to business study. It provides a broad understanding of business fundamentals and prepares you for progression to Level 4 study or entry-level employment.

What You Will Learn

Mandatory units

  • Introduction to Research and Academic Skills
  • Using IT for Business
  • Introduction to Economics and Accounting
  • Introduction to the Business Environment

Optional units (choose two)

  • Introduction to Customer Service
  • Managing Business Events
  • Introduction to Marketing
  • Introduction to People Management

Unit titles are as published by OTHM. The full unit table with official references and credit values is below.

Study Hours, Entry and Assessment

Guided learning hours (GLH): 360
Total qualification time (TQT): 600
Entry requirements: Open to learners aged 16 and over. OTHM does not specify formal entry requirements for Level 3 qualifications. If English is not your first language, you will need evidence of English language competency.
Assessment: All units are internally assessed by the centre and externally verified by OTHM. Assessment is coursework-based — written assignments against published criteria, with no examinations. Grading is Pass/Fail.
Programme status: This is a prospective programme, subject to OTHM centre approval. Enrolment will open once approval is granted.

Progression Routes

  • Progress to Level 4 - Continue to an OTHM Level 4 Diploma in Business Management.
  • Enter employment - Use your qualification for entry-level roles in administration, customer service, sales, or retail.
  • Enter the first year of a degree - Progress directly into Year 1 of a UK bachelor's degree programme.
