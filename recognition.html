Who is OTHM?

OTHM is a UK-based awarding organisation. It designs qualifications — including the diplomas from Level 3 to Level 7 described on this site — and approves centres around the world to deliver them. When you complete an OTHM programme, your certificate is issued by OTHM itself, and it is identical to those issued to learners at any other OTHM centre worldwide.

Official website: othm.org.uk

Who regulates OTHM? Meet Ofqual

Ofqual — the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation — is the independent regulator of qualifications and examinations in England. OTHM is recognised by Ofqual, which means its qualifications must meet strict standards for design, delivery, assessment and certification. Recognition by Ofqual is what makes an OTHM diploma a regulated qualification rather than just a certificate of attendance.

What is the RQF?

The Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) organises regulated qualifications into 9 levels, so employers and universities can compare them at a glance. Every OTHM diploma on this site sits on the RQF at the level in its title:

How to verify a qualification yourself

Don't take our word for it — check the public registers:

1. Verify the qualification: search the Ofqual Register of Regulated Qualifications for the exact qualification title, e.g. “OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership”.
2. Verify the awarding organisation: confirm OTHM appears on Ofqual's list of recognised awarding organisations.
3. Verify the centre: once we are approved, our centre will be listed by OTHM. Until then, our status is candidate centre — see below.

Recognition questions, answered

Is OTHM a recognised awarding organisation?

Yes. OTHM is recognised and regulated by Ofqual in England, and its qualifications sit on the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF).

How can I verify an OTHM qualification?

Search the Ofqual Register of Regulated Qualifications at register.ofqual.gov.uk for the qualification title. You can also confirm a centre's status on OTHM's own website.

What does candidate-centre status mean for me?

We are preparing our OTHM centre application. Programmes are prospective until approval is granted — nothing is delivered or charged before then.

Will my certificate be issued by OTHM?

Yes. Once approved, certificates are issued by OTHM and are identical to those issued at any other OTHM centre worldwide.
