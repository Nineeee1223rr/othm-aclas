## 1. OTHM Level 3 Diploma in Business Studies

- **Official title:** OTHM Level 3 Diploma in Business Studies
- **Page URL:** http://othm.org.uk/qualification/othm-level-3-diploma-in-business-studies
- **Spec PDF:** https://othm.org.uk/docs/qualifications/othm-level-3-diploma-in-business-studies/specification_files/othm_l3_dip_bs_spec_2025docx_2025-05-20_16-29.pdf (May 2025 spec)
- **Credits:** 60 (4 mandatory × 10 + choose 2 optional × 10)
- **GLH:** 360 (60 per unit) · **TQT:** 600 (100 per unit)
- **Units (mandatory):**
  | Unit ref | Unit title | Credits | GLH | TQT |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | R/616/0686 | Introduction to Research and Academic Skills | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | J/616/2712 | Using IT for Business | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | D/616/0691 | Introduction to Economics and Accounting | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | L/616/2713 | Introduction to the Business Environment | 10 | 60 | 100 |
- **Units (optional — choose any TWO):**
  | Unit ref | Unit title | Credits | GLH | TQT |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | H/616/0692 | Introduction to Customer Service | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | R/616/2714 | Managing Business Events | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | D/616/2716 | Introduction to Marketing | 10 | 60 | 100 |
  | K/616/2718 | Introduction to People Management | 10 | 60 | 100 |
- **Entry requirements (OTHM official):** "OTHM Level 3 qualifications can be offered to learners from age 16. OTHM does not specify entry requirements for these qualifications." English competency required if not from a majority English-speaking country.
- **Assessment:** Internally assessed by the centre, externally verified by OTHM; criterion-referenced; Pass judgement by Assessor against all learning outcomes/assessment criteria. (Spec PDFs list Assessment Methods: Coursework.)
- **Duration:** 6 months full-time; flexible part-time/distance. Delivery: face-to-face, lectures, tutorials, seminars, distance, online (approved centres).
- **Equivalence:** GCE AS/A Levels. **Progression:** direct entry into Year 1 of a UK bachelor's degree, or Level 4 diplomas.

## Cross-qualification notes

- **Assessment wording (official, identical across all pages):** "All units within this qualification are internally assessed by the centre and externally verified by OTHM. The qualifications are criterion referenced, based on the achievement of all the specified learning outcomes." Spec PDFs list Assessment Methods as **Coursework** (assignment-based; no exams) and grading as Pass/Fail.
- **Delivery wording (official):** "delivered face-to-face, through lectures, tutorials, seminars, distance, online by approved centres only."
- **English requirements (official):** "If a learner is not from a majority English-speaking country, they must provide evidence of English language competency."
- **Ages:** L3 from 16 (no specified entry requirements); L4/L5/L6 typically 18+; L7 must be 21+ at course start.
- **Items NOT verified from official pages:** unit reference numbers for the six L5 IT units (names only, from the extended diploma page); exact Ofqual qualification numbers (not needed for course pages).
